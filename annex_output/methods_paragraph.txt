Screening and data extraction were performed by a local two-model ensemble served through Ollama: gemma4:e4b (8.0B, Q4_K_M) and llama3:latest (8.0B, Q4_0), at temperature 0 with fixed seed 42 and a schema-constrained JSON response format. Records were classified under a union decision rule (include if any model in any run included) with 2 runs per model per record. The eligibility criteria were frozen before searching and passed verbatim into the prompt; the criteria block is hashed (xxhash64 e021124a22ce6e9d) and the hash is asserted at every run. Prompt polarity was selected on benchmark data, not on our corpus: three logically equivalent phrasings (affirmative inclusion, antonymic exclusion, predicate negation) were compared on the SYNERGY benchmark dataset Bannach-Brown_2019, and the affirmative phrasing restricted to abstract-present records was deployed. That configuration achieved sensitivity 1.000 (exact 95% CI 0.753-1.000 on 13 positive records) and specificity 0.926, with precision 0.650. Inter-model agreement on our corpus was Cohen's kappa 0.343 (raw agreement 0.9929); 434 of 550 included records rested on a single model's vote. Extraction was schema-constrained to the methods and results sections of GROBID-produced TEI where full text was obtainable and to title and abstract otherwise, with the evidence base recorded per value. Every non-null extracted value carried a verbatim evidence quote that was string-matched back against the source text; values whose quote could not be found were set to missing and the per-field grounding rate is reported as a directly measured hallucination rate. All prompts, model names, quantisation, temperatures and seeds are reported in the supplementary material.

All classification and extraction were performed by automated systems; no human reviewer screened records or extracted data. Classifier performance was validated against external labelled benchmarks prior to application, and all extracted fields were verified by verbatim quote-matching against source text. Performance metrics are reported in full; fields falling below reliability thresholds were excluded from analysis.
